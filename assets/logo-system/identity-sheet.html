Do & don't
Do
Keep the accent on the nucleus only · maintain clearspace · use the dark lockup on dark grounds · use one-color versions for print/fax/engraving · let the mark sit on calm backgrounds.
Don't
Don't recolor the wordmark with the accent · don't rotate, stretch, or restyle the symbol · don't add gradients, shadows, or outlines · don't place on busy imagery without a solid backing · don't swap the typeface.

Same geometry, one accent, and light/dark parity carry the identity across every surface — from a browser tab to a signed institutional report — so EthosFi reads as one recognizable company, not a concept.
